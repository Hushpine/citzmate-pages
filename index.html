Q: Does CitzMate require an internet connection?
No. CitzMate is 100% standalone and offline. All 600 questions, plain-English explanations, and audio speech work without Wi-Fi or cellular data.
Q: How do I change speech audio speed?
On the home screen, tap the speaker icon in the top-right corner to adjust between slow (0.75x) and standard (1.0x) speeds, or select alternative English voices.
Q: Are the questions up to date for 2026?
Yes. All questions are aligned with the official Department of Home Affairs handbook "Australian Citizenship: Our Common Bond", including all mandatory Australian Values questions.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

At CitzMate, we believe your privacy is fundamental. Our application is architected from the ground up to respect your personal privacy:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your name, email, IP address, device location, or personal information.
  • No Accounts Required: You can use all features immediately without registering an account or logging in.
  • Local Storage: Your study progress, test scores, bookmarks, and mistake logs are stored purely on your local device.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising: CitzMate contains zero advertising tracking frameworks or commercial trackers.
